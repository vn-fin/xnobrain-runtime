# Provider asset attribution

## OpenCode

`opencode.svg` is the official OpenCode light square logo downloaded from the
[OpenCode brand guidelines](https://opencode.ai/brand). The original project is
distributed under the [MIT License](https://github.com/anomalyco/opencode/blob/dev/LICENSE).
The SVG was reformatted without changing its paths, colors, or view box.

## xAI and Groq

`xai.svg` and `groq.svg` are sourced from
[LobeHub Lobe Icons 1.91.0](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons), an AI
provider icon collection distributed under the MIT License. Their monochrome
paths are rendered in white to match the existing provider-card treatment.
