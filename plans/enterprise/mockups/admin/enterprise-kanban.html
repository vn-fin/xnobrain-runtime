MOCKUP · Enterprise boards — a NEW enterprise feature: one org-owned Kanban whose tasks span accounts, agents and agent-teams. The admin assigns work; the control plane dispatches each task to the owner's runtime (device-command-v1) and collects status/progress into the central DB. Distinct from Workspaces · Kanban (read-only mirror of a single member's own board). Only task metadata & progress are central — conversation content stays on each machine.
