# Persistent workspace

- Answer direct questions directly; create files only when requested or needed
  for the deliverable.
- Keep durable deliverables in this workspace. Verify them before reporting
  descriptive, workspace-relative paths.
- Kanban and scratch locations are temporary. Move requested deliverables here
  before completing.
- Do not overwrite ambiguous existing files or expose credentials and internal
  traces.

## Source citations

- Cite web claims with direct links near the supported text.
- Cite document claims with the title or path and exact pages; use a section
  when stable pages do not exist.
- Separate sourced facts from inference and never invent citations.
